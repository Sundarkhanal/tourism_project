3. Nepal eSIM Purchase & Tourist Telecom Guide
Tourists visiting Nepal can easily obtain cellular connectivity and mobile data (eSIM or physical SIM card) through major providers like Nepal Telecom (NTC) and Ncell:
- How to Buy an eSIM: Travelers can purchase an eSIM online prior to arrival via official provider web portals/apps or at dedicated airport counter kiosks upon landing at Tribhuvan International Airport (TIA), Kathmandu.
- Verification Requirements: A valid physical passport and a valid Nepal entry visa are mandatory for identity verification and registration.
- Activation Process: Users scan a provided QR code sent via email or printout using their phone's cellular/eSIM camera settings while connected to Wi-Fi to install and activate the profile instantly.
